About Hi-Tech Ghaziabad

Hi-Tech Institute of Engineering & Technology (HIET) is a premier engineering college established in Ghaziabad, Uttar Pradesh along the Delhi-Hapur Bypass (NH-24). The institute aims to provide top-quality technical education, research opportunities, and skill development to students in the Delhi NCR region.
