About HIET Ghaziabad

Hi-Tech Institute of Engineering & Technology (HIET) is a premier engineering institution located in Ghaziabad, Uttar Pradesh. Approved by AICTE and affiliated with Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam Technical University (AKTU), HIET provides quality technical and managerial education to empower young minds with knowledge and skills.
